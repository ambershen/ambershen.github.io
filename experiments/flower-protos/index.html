Flower prototypes

A

Open from the start

Every word is visible at once. The breeze plays by itself, and a caption under the flower names each effect.

 B

Scroll story

Each line of the introduction takes over the flower as you scroll. The flower stays pinned and playable.

 C

Specimen viewer

One flower, six plates. It tours the effects by itself; touch it and the tour pauses.

 D

Story, stickers and water

B and C together: scroll the introduction, words circle the flower as stickers, a ghost brush plays when you don’t, and the flower sinks into water where the selected work surfaces.
